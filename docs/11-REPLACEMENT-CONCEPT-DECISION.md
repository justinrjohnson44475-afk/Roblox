November 8, 2026. Supersedes the Harborlight selection in document 02. Sources and observation limits are in document 10. Scores are judgments, not measured demand. Higher is favorable; weights sum to 100.

Creature expedition: collect and evolve a companion with ability combinations; strong visible transformation but requires many animated creatures and crowded collection competition. Scrap mech arena: modular combat chassis and PvP skill; compelling silhouette changes, but match population, balance and modular physics are expensive. Skyship raiders: cooperative boarding and crew progression; boats/airships risk a broad physics scope and less direct character identity. Relic diving: equipment-dependent traversal and depth hazards; distinctive exploration but underwater feel and content need substantial validation.

Riftwake wins provisionally because PvE build requests, boss-pattern praise, control overload and deterministic-upgrade complaints map to a compact actionable slice. These sources do not establish a large addressable audience. We are implementing three authored regions and a single evolving rig before proposing an MMO, generated universe or massive class roster.

## Fantasy and distinguishing action
Start with a fractured relic gauntlet. Enter floating storm ruins. Fight constructs with readable charged attacks. Build Focus with precise strikes, use a Coreburst to break a charge, and exploit the exposed core. Earn shards, levels and boss cores; forge larger armor/gauntlet silhouettes, unlock abilities, and choose Vanguard, Strider or Arcanist configurations with different combat tradeoffs. The character's options, appearance and reachable world must change together.

## Why each feature belongs
- Strike combo: immediate agency and a compact learnable attack rhythm.
- Dash: positioning and a timed defensive tool, not a sold advantage.
- Coreburst: spend earned combat Focus to interrupt a charge; creates a choice between damage now and a safer opening.
- Overdrive: later earned area ability, a visible capability milestone.
- Forge upgrades: known prices, known effects, permanent progress.
- Three free build paths: experiment with survivability, mobility and ability use without paid resets.
- Three regional wardens: progression gates prove combat success instead of only elapsed grind.
- Participant rewards: teammates help without stealing the final hit.
- Sanctuary reading: explicit voluntary Christian content, no power/currency/quest connection.

## What would falsify the idea
Players ignore break windows and win by standing still; upgrades feel like the same action with bigger numbers; early controls are confusing; the first boss requires repetitive farming; regions look interchangeable; apparent cosmetics are unreadable on mobile. Address these before producing more regions. The long-term goal is a high-quality live game; calling this a masterpiece now would repeat the earlier mistake.
