# Biblical integration and safeguards

A clearly marked “Optional Christian reading — Garden of Hope” sign opens a local reading panel only when a player deliberately interacts. Entering the garden does not auto-open content. Close is always available. The main repair loop, all currency, cosmetics, progression and social recognition work independently. No reward, badge, declaration, religious profile, attendance record or reading analytics exists.

The panel uses original explanatory summaries, not Bible quotations. It identifies Christian teaching explicitly: God loves the world (John 3:16); Jesus Christ died for sins and rose again (1 Corinthians 15:3–4); salvation is God’s gift through faith, not earned by works (Ephesians 2:8–9). Faith is not presented as a game transaction. Kindness and repairing the harbor do not earn salvation. No statement is required from the reader.

Matthew 22:37–39 informs love of God and neighbor; Luke 10:25–37 informs compassion across social boundaries; Micah 6:8 informs justice, mercy and humility; Matthew 5:14–16 informs visible good works; 1 Peter 3:15 informs gentleness and respect; 1 Corinthians 13 informs love as more than outward performance. Gameplay is an illustration of care, not a replacement for these passages’ meanings. A qualified Christian reviewer should review summaries in context before release. No claim of theological review has been made.

## Permissions and player safety
No full translation text is included. Before adding quotations, select a translation, verify rights for the relevant jurisdictions, retain permission/attribution evidence and review every quote. Do not assume every translation is public domain worldwide.

There is no free-text religious discussion UI, prayer request collection, off-platform contact, conversion funnel or player-to-player spiritual counseling feature. Use Roblox’s own reporting/moderation tools for ordinary interaction. Do not target advertising by inferred belief or reward religious acts. Respect everyone’s decision to close or ignore the panel.

## Current platform review gate
The [current maturity guidance](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/8862768451604-Content-Maturity-Ratings) includes sensitive religious themes under 16+ content. Whether this particular optional reading meets that definition requires an accurate Maturity & Compliance questionnaire and, if uncertain, Roblox clarification. Optional placement does not exempt content from review. Do not promise Minimal/under-13 availability. Declare the complete experience honestly; do not hide content or use a client toggle to evade ratings. Read the [Community Standards](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/203313410-Roblox-Community-Standards/) and [Advertising Standards](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/13722260778260-Advertising-Standards) before publication or promotion. Reviewed November 7, 2026; recheck before launch.

QA must confirm there are no economy or analytics calls on the reading path and that multiplayer players cannot force another player’s panel open. Compare gameplay with and without reading; rewards must be identical for identical actions.
