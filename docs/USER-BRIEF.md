## YOUR ROLE

You are to function as a complete, highly experienced Roblox game development studio.

Assume the combined responsibilities of:

- Senior Roblox Game Developer and Luau Engineer
- Game Director and Creative Director
- Gaming Industry Market Research Analyst
- Competitive Intelligence and Product Strategy Analyst
- Game Economist and Monetization Strategist

Your mission is to **research, design, develop, test, document, and prepare for launch an original Roblox game with strong, evidence-based commercial potential, high replayability, excellent player experience, and an intentional yet nonintrusive opportunity to introduce interested players to the gospel of Jesus Christ.**

Treat this as a serious entrepreneurial venture with real business objectives.

# PHASE 1: COMPREHENSIVE ROBLOX MARKET RESEARCH

Before writing the game itself, conduct a professional competitive intelligence investigation of the Roblox ecosystem.

Research the top 15–20 relevant Roblox games, including:

1. Current leading games by concurrent players.
2. Games with exceptional total visits and sustained popularity.
3. Games showing strong recent growth.
4. Games with enduring player retention and communities.
5. Games with commercially effective monetization systems.
6. Emerging games that may indicate changing player preferences.

Use the latest information available when the research is performed. Do not assume historical leaders are still current leaders.

Prioritize official Roblox sources, Roblox Creator Hub, publicly available analytics, developer interviews, reputable industry reports, and credible player communities.

For each game, investigate:

- Game name, developer/studio, and genre.
- Release date and growth history, where verifiable.
- Publicly available visits, favorites, concurrent player counts, and other engagement indicators.
- Target player segments and motivations.
- Core gameplay loop.
- Progression systems.
- Reward structures and pacing.
- Social features and multiplayer mechanics.
- Reasons players return daily or weekly.
- Content update frequency.
- User-generated or emergent gameplay opportunities.
- Game economy and monetization design.
- Onboarding and first-session experience.
- Visual design, audio, atmosphere, and accessibility.
- Community-building and discoverability strategies.
- Positive and negative player feedback.
- Major weaknesses, frustrations, and unmet player expectations.
- Potential reasons for popularity or decline.

### Reverse-engineering requirement

Reverse-engineer the observable success principles of these games, not their proprietary code or intellectual property.

Determine **why their gameplay systems work psychologically, socially, technically, creatively, and commercially**.

Distinguish between:

- Evidence-supported causes of success.
- Correlations that do not demonstrate causation.
- Reasonable hypotheses.
- Unverified claims.

Never invent revenue figures, retention rates, conversion rates, or financial results.

Where financial information is unavailable, clearly state that and develop transparent, assumption-based scenarios instead.

Use only legitimate public research methods and authorized tools. Do not exploit games, bypass security, steal code, copy protected assets, or violate platform terms.

### Required deliverable

Create a professionally written `docs/01-MARKET-RESEARCH.md` document containing:

- Executive summary.
- Research methodology and date.
- Competitive landscape.
- Individual game analyses.
- Comparative evaluation matrix.
- Monetization findings.
- Player psychology and retention findings.
- Market trends.
- Identified weaknesses.
- Source links and citations.
- Evidence limitations.
- Strategic conclusions.

# PHASE 2: IDENTIFY THE MOST PROFITABLE MARKET OPPORTUNITY

Using the research, identify genuine gaps or underserved opportunities in Roblox.

Do not simply suggest a game because its genre is currently popular.

Search for opportunities at the intersection of:

1. Strong demonstrated player demand.
2. Dissatisfaction with existing game experiences.
3. Inadequately served player motivations.
4. Potential for distinctive and memorable gameplay.
5. Natural social engagement and word-of-mouth growth.
6. Long-term content expansion.
7. Ethical monetization potential.
8. Reasonable technical and financial development requirements.
9. Opportunity to incorporate meaningful Christian themes without diminishing mainstream appeal.

Identify at least five distinct game concepts.

Evaluate each using a weighted decision matrix covering:

- Market demand.
- Competitive saturation.
- Degree of differentiation.
- Development complexity.
- Cost and maintenance.
- Monetization suitability.
- Retention potential.
- Social and viral potential.
- Long-term scalability.
- Compatibility with my broader mission.

Explain why each score was assigned and identify its supporting evidence.

Select the strongest concept, clearly defending your decision.

### Critical strategic instruction

Do not blindly combine every mechanic from successful Roblox games into one incoherent experience.

Instead, extract the **underlying principles** behind their success and integrate only the systems that reinforce the selected game's identity.

For every meaningful feature, explain which player need it serves and why it belongs in the game.

If research reveals weaknesses in leading games, design deliberate improvements addressing those weaknesses.

The finished concept should be recognizably original and offer players a compelling reason to choose it over existing alternatives.

### Required deliverable

Create `docs/02-MARKET-GAP-AND-OPPORTUNITY.md`.

Include the five concepts, scoring model, selected concept, unique selling proposition, commercial rationale, competitive advantages, risks, and evidence.

# PHASE 3: INCORPORATE THE GOSPEL OF JESUS CHRIST

This requirement is deeply important.

I want the game to be commercially viable and appealing to a broad Roblox audience, while creating genuine opportunities for interested players to encounter the gospel of Jesus Christ.

The gospel must be approached with **biblical accuracy, wisdom, tact, kindness, respect, and intentionality**.

The intention is not to trick, pressure, shame, or manipulate players into religious engagement.

Instead, create a game world in which Christian values and optional opportunities for exploring the gospel exist naturally and meaningfully.

## Biblical foundation

Ground the Christian narrative and messaging in Scripture, particularly principles involving:

- The love of God — John 3:16.
- Salvation through Jesus Christ — Ephesians 2:8–9.
- The death and resurrection of Jesus Christ — 1 Corinthians 15:3–4.
- Loving God and one's neighbor — Matthew 22:37–39.
- Compassion, mercy, and kindness — Luke 10:25–37.
- Integrity and doing what is right — Micah 6:8.
- Being a light to others — Matthew 5:14–16.
- Giving a reason for one's faith with gentleness and respect — 1 Peter 3:15.
- The importance of love — 1 Corinthians 13.

Scripture should be interpreted carefully and in context. Do not misrepresent biblical passages merely to make gameplay fit.

Verify Bible quotations and translation permissions before incorporating their full text into the game.

## Design expectations

Explore creative approaches such as:

- An optional sanctuary, garden, chapel, library, or peaceful discovery area.
- Non-player characters whose kindness and actions demonstrate Christian virtues.
- Optional conversations about hope, forgiveness, sacrifice, grace, and redemption.
- Meaningful stories that allow players to experience moral decisions without compulsory religious outcomes.
- Optional narrative quests that explore biblical themes.
- An explicitly labeled, voluntary space where interested players can learn what the gospel teaches about Jesus Christ.
- Natural storytelling that encourages curiosity rather than confrontation.

These are possibilities, not mandatory mechanics. Select the approaches that best fit the game.

### Essential boundaries

1. The main game must remain fully enjoyable without engaging in religious content.
2. Religious participation must never be required for progression, rankings, currency, or access to core experiences.
3. Do not reward religious declarations, professions of faith, or participation with competitive advantages.
4. Never use fear, shame, guilt, or emotional pressure to encourage religious engagement.
5. Respect players of different faiths and those with no religious affiliation.
6. Make explicitly Christian material recognizable and optional rather than concealing it to manipulate players.
7. Do not collect personal religious information or create unsafe interactions involving minors.
8. Follow all current Roblox content rules, age-appropriateness requirements, and community standards.

The goal is to **create an enjoyable game first, while leaving an authentic, purposeful, biblically grounded invitation for willing players to explore the gospel**.

### Required deliverable

Create `docs/03-BIBLICAL-INTEGRATION-STRATEGY.md`, explaining the theological foundation, player experience, optional gameplay integration, ethical safeguards, and platform compliance.

# PHASE 5: DESIGN AN ETHICAL, PROFITABLE BUSINESS MODEL

This game is intended to become a legitimate, revenue-generating business.

Profitability must be actively considered throughout its design rather than added as an afterthought.

Research current Roblox monetization systems, creator earnings arrangements, platform fees, DevEx requirements, relevant regional payout eligibility, and available analytics.

Verify official requirements before building projections.

## Monetization research

Evaluate appropriate options, potentially including:

- Cosmetic items.
- Character customization.
- Premium visual effects.
- Optional game passes.
- Developer products.
- Nonessential convenience enhancements.
- Special themed content.
- Social customization.
- Other monetization mechanics permitted by current Roblox policy.

Determine which monetization methods best complement the actual game concept.

Do not introduce monetization merely because competitors use it.

### Ethical requirements

Avoid exploitative mechanics, deceptive purchases, compulsive spending pressure, and predatory design, especially because Roblox includes younger players.

Do not make purchasing essential to enjoy or complete the core game.

Do not sell religious participation, access to the gospel, prayer, spiritual rewards, or salvation-related content.

Keep in-game purchases clearly explained and compliant with Roblox policies.

## Financial strategy

Create a financial model with:

- Estimated initial development expenses.
- Monthly operating costs.
- Realistic ongoing maintenance requirements.
- Revenue sources.
- Pricing hypotheses.
- Conversion-rate assumptions.
- Average revenue per paying user.
- Player acquisition assumptions.
- Revenue scenarios.
- Break-even analysis.
- Profitability scenarios.
- Financial risks.

Provide conservative, base-case, and optimistic scenarios.

Separate verified market data from assumptions.

Never guarantee that a game will become profitable.

Develop a practical strategy for achieving sustainable profit by improving genuine player enjoyment and value.

### Analytics

Specify key measurements, such as:

- Daily and monthly active users.
- New player onboarding completion.
- Session length.
- Day 1, Day 7, and Day 30 retention.
- Engagement and progression.
- Purchases and conversion rates.
- Average revenue per paying user.
- User acquisition and discoverability.
- Player satisfaction.
- Performance and crash rates.

Use analytics in a privacy-conscious, age-appropriate way.
