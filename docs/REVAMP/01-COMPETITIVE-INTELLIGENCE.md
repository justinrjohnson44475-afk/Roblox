## Retention hypotheses to test

Mastery should change which threats a player can handle; earned upgrades should change visible equipment; regional discoveries should change available routes. A successful first session should show the next attainable state and provide a satisfying stopping point. These are hypotheses. Public CCU and self-selected posts cannot establish a causal retention effect; compare actual cohorts after authorized instrumentation, without tracking optional reading.
