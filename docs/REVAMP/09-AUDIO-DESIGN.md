# Audio design

Fourteen original mono22,050Hz PCM WAV sketches are generated by `scripts/ascension/author_audio.py`; no samples, borrowed melodies or external licenses. Cues: miss, block, parry, light, heavy, exposed, corebreak, ground, stone/grass footstep, Warden; three tonal exploration/combat/boss loops. These are sound-design drafts, not mastered production audio. Source integrity validates their format, not perceived quality.

Client Audio creates Music/Effects/Ambience buses and capped positional voice anchors (32 desktop/16 mobile). Voices expire3s and anchors return to a bounded reuse pool. Effects respect rolloff. Music switches exploration/boss in the current slice. Asset IDs are0; consequently no new sounds play in the diagnostic place until legitimate upload/configuration. This is visible in readiness, and no substitute ping pretends to be the final audio.

Remaining: owned sound uploads, mix review on speakers/headphones/mobile, streaming/preload/failure behavior, smooth crossfades/combat layer, regional wind/water/foliage ambience, distinct enemy windup/recovery sounds, and surface footsteps hooked to authored FootLeft/FootRight markers plus floor raycasts. Protect core warnings from masking; provide independent bus sliders/mute and avoid sound-only danger. A permanent cosmetic pass must not alter threat audibility.
