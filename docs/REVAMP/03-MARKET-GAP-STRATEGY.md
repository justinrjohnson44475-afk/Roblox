# Market-gap strategy

The differentiated promise is **earn a visibly stronger character by reading and breaking a dangerous PvE opponent**, together or alone. The signature decision is guard/parry for ordinary strikes versus evasion or a committed Corebreak for marked charges. Core exposure then makes attack commitment rewarding. This is a product hypothesis, not a proven unserved market.

The short loop is observe → defend/break → exploit exposure → earn → forge. The medium loop is unlock a guaranteed blade, earn Coreburst/Overdrive, evolve a silhouette and open the next region. The long loop is master distinct wardens, discover optional routes and explore freely switchable build tradeoffs. Existing IDs/balances survive. No paid power, paid resets, PvP, firearms, shards for Robux, premium currency or paid consumable products.

The market is crowded with combat RPGs. Listing counts cannot justify creating another bandit grind. Therefore the initial investment is one complete Warden slice, not three unfinished worlds. Success requires observed control feel, understandable threats and enjoyment before map/content expansion. A failure to reach the study targets triggers redesign of input, charge communication or enemy pacing, not a larger reward grind.

The source checkpoint improves architecture and supplies original drafts. It does not yet substantiate a polished distinctive game, competitive parity, retention, revenue or commercial success. Production art, animation review, tested multiplayer and independent studies are still necessary.
