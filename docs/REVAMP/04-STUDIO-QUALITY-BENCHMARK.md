# Studio-quality benchmark

Scale anchors apply consistently to every category: **1 broken; 2 functional with major deficiencies; 3 coherent but visibly unfinished; 4 meets documented targets with independent evidence; 5 demonstrably exceeds the reference standard**. Missing observation is **Not Evaluated**, never zero and never an inferred three. Do not compute a composite from an incomplete set. Final release requires ≥4 in every category, not merely a high average.

| Category | Weight | Evidence required for 4 | Current score |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Player movement and control responsiveness | 18% | First local response within one render frame; continuous wall-safe dash; strafing, jump/landing and input parity; capture input-to-visible latency at 0/100/200ms. | Not Evaluated |

Jujutsu Shenanigans is the destruction/readability reference; The Strongest Battlegrounds is the movement/melee choreography reference; RIVALS is the input/camera/feedback reference. They are relevant action references, not a verified top-three worldwide ranking. Reference scores also remain Not Evaluated until observed. Compare a matched input task, camera distance, encounter duration and device; record reference build/date and evaluator identity. A five requires independent comparative evidence beyond meeting our own target.
