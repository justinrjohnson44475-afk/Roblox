# Roblox market research — initial decision dossier

## Executive summary
Public evidence supports demand for collection, visible progression and shared experiences. It does not establish the profitability of a new restoration game. We select a cooperative harbor restoration slice as a testable commercial hypothesis: retain clear collection feedback and social identity while avoiding theft, opaque grind and paid power.

## Method and evidence boundaries
Research performed November 7, 2026, client date. Reviewed a dated third-party chart [S1], cross-checked directionally against a second chart [S2], inspected twenty official Roblox descriptions, and reviewed official monetization, analytics, discovery and safety guidance. The chart reported a November 7 22:15 UTC snapshot. Counts below are **third-party reports**, not directly sampled Roblox API measurements. Source selection has survivorship and popularity bias. A single CCU snapshot cannot establish growth, retention or monetization effectiveness. Charts disagree in time, filters, developer labels and counts. No proprietary code, private analytics or assets were obtained.

Each official listing is linked below. Description evidence supports advertised mechanics, not verified runtime quality. Static listings sometimes say “no running experiences”; that dynamic placeholder cannot establish zero CCU. Release dates, favorites, exact update histories, player demographics, real revenue, conversion and retention are **not verified for any game**. Do not substitute place creation dates for release dates. Do not extrapolate financial results from popularity.

## Individual analyses

### Steal An Egg
[Official listing](https://www.roblox.com/games/107778070777162) — creator: and Collect Rare Pets; Simulation. [S1] reported 759,028 concurrent players.

**Advertised loop:** Steal eggs, hatch pets, earn income, improve base and speed. **Interpretation:** Collection, escalating upgrades and rivalry. This is a plausible return motive, not a measured daily/weekly retention result. **Concern:** Unpredictable loss may generate stories but also frustration. **Design implication:** Protect permanent progress; recover supplies instead of stealing.


### Brookhaven RP
[Official listing](https://www.roblox.com/games/4924922222) — creator: Brookhaven by Voldex; Roleplay. [S1] reported 464,344 concurrent players.

**Advertised loop:** Choose homes, vehicles and roles; improvise with friends. **Interpretation:** Self-expression and player-authored stories. This is a plausible return motive, not a measured daily/weekly retention result. **Concern:** Open-ended onboarding can lack a concrete first objective. **Design implication:** Offer a clear first repair alongside a welcoming shared place.


### Blox Fruits
[Official listing](https://www.roblox.com/games/2753915549) — creator: Gamer Robot Inc; Action RPG. [S1] reported 333,756 concurrent players.

**Advertised loop:** Fight, grow stronger, travel and discover abilities. **Interpretation:** Mastery and visible long-range goals. This is a plausible return motive, not a measured daily/weekly retention result. **Concern:** A large progression ladder can become repetitive; not measured here. **Design implication:** Show the next repair goal immediately; avoid power inflation.


### Murder Mystery 2
[Official listing](https://www.roblox.com/games/142823291) — creator: Nikilis; Social survival. [S1] reported 305,150 concurrent players.

**Advertised loop:** Survive changing hidden roles; collect and trade knives. **Interpretation:** Uncertainty, short rounds and collection identity. This is a plausible return motive, not a measured daily/weekly retention result. **Concern:** Elimination downtime and trade risk are design concerns. **Design implication:** Keep contribution possible throughout a round; omit trading.


### Throw Food For Animals
[Official listing](https://www.roblox.com/games/120219252362013) — creator: Goofy Chupacabra; Collection simulation. [S1] reported 214,920 concurrent players.

**Advertised loop:** Throw food, attract animals, earn and train throwing power. **Interpretation:** Readable action-to-upgrade feedback. This is a plausible return motive, not a measured daily/weekly retention result. **Concern:** Depth beyond the initial novelty remains unverified. **Design implication:** Make each recovered supply alter a visible shared project.


### Adopt Me!
[Official listing](https://www.roblox.com/games/920587237) — creator: Uplift Games; Pet roleplay. [S1] reported 214,619 concurrent players.

**Advertised loop:** Raise and collect pets, build homes, trade and roleplay. **Interpretation:** Attachment, care and social display. This is a plausible return motive, not a measured daily/weekly retention result. **Concern:** Developer safety materials document scam patterns [S5]. **Design implication:** Use earned cosmetic identity without player-to-player transfers.


### RIVALS

### Coverage limits applying to every analysis
Audience motivations, reward pacing, first-session friction, emergent play and visual/audio quality require direct observation. Advertised cooperative or roleplay mechanics support a social-play interpretation; they do not measure intentional co-play. Specific monetization prices, current inventories, tutorial durations, accessibility settings and community sentiment have not been audited. Official listings advertise seasonal additions in several games, but only BedWars explicitly advertised a weekly Friday cadence when read. Do not assume all games update weekly. Follow-up research must capture store pages, dated changelogs, device observations and a broader feedback sample per game.

## Player feedback and unmet needs
Uplift’s own safety guidance documents trading scams [S5]; it does not prove a scam prevalence rate. A Fisch discussion [S6] reports grind and unclear requirements; a Brainrot thread [S7] reports loss and paid-power frustration. These self-selected anecdotes are signals for interviews, not representative satisfaction scores. Positive appeal is inferred from described agency, collection and social play; we have no representative positive/negative survey. Counter-hypothesis: players may actively prefer competitive theft, so removing it may reduce excitement for that segment.

## Psychology, monetization and trends
Collection creates legible goals; ownership supports self-expression; short shared objectives may reduce coordination costs. These are design hypotheses. The co-occurrence of these systems in popular games is correlation, not causal proof. A historical gardening peak [S8] and the current chart illustrate why peaks should not be treated as enduring baselines. Recent high-ranking egg variants show competitive saturation as well as demand. Seasonal descriptions suggest novelty as an acquisition tool, but incremental impact is unknown.

Official Roblox discovery guidance [S3] emphasizes lasting value, early bounce, repeat play, playtime and co-play alongside spending. Prioritize understandable first actions and satisfying returns over padded session time. Monetization channels are documented, but competitors’ actual revenue is unavailable. Our commercial decision uses transparent scenarios in document 07, not invented competitor earnings.

## Strategic conclusion and next research
Proceed with a low-cost, reversible slice. Test whether visible cooperative restoration attracts repeat play and cosmetic interest. Before paid acquisition: audit at least five close restoration/adventure competitors, refresh official counts and favorites, sample multiple days, observe onboarding on real devices, interview actual testers and complete policy review. Do not call the proposed niche underserved until audience evidence supports that claim.
