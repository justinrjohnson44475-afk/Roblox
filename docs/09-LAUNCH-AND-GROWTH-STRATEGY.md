# Launch and sustainable growth

## Positioning and store copy (draft)
**Riftwake: Ascend**

“A fractured gauntlet. A world above the storm. Break enemy charges, expose their cores and forge the combat rig you want to become. Earn new abilities, shape your build and challenge three regional wardens solo or together. Includes a clearly labeled optional Christian reading garden with no rewards. Early development build; combat, visuals and services are still being tested.”

Only advertise implemented gameplay. Do not claim player numbers, awards, an age rating, limited offers or release dates without evidence. Current audience assumption is teen/adult cozy-coop players; actual eligibility depends on Roblox classification. Do not hide religious content from the listing or maturity questionnaire.

## Creative briefs
Icon: an original evolved gauntlet framing the broken Astral halo, sea-glass energy, strong silhouette and no illegible text. Thumbnail A: two actual player avatars breaking a warden’s charged attack. Thumbnail B: truthful in-game before/after of the earned rig evolution. Produce captures only after the world is visually verified; concept art must not be presented as gameplay. Asset rights and moderation are release gates.

Short videos: a genuine Fractured-to-Awakened transformation; a two-player core break; a calm build-path showcase. Do not fake social proof, earnings, reward luck or charitable impact. Do not use religious pressure as an acquisition hook.

## Discovery and acquisition evidence
[Official discovery guidance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery) and [FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq), read November 7, 2026, discuss early bounce, playthrough, repeat play, playtime, co-play and spending among organic Home users. Improve title/thumbnail accuracy and first-session clarity; do not pad time. Ad acquisition and organic recommendation signals are distinct. Measure cohorts separately; no guaranteed ranking tactic exists.

Start with a private playtest and transparent Roblox community posts where allowed. Publish update notes, use legitimate platform event/notification tools and respond to issues. Collaborate with small creators who actually enjoy PvE character building; disclose sponsorships and agree on honest footage. No messages, paid campaigns, influencer outreach or community accounts have been created in this task.

Keep discussion on age-appropriate Roblox surfaces with moderation/reporting. Do not route minors to private DMs, collect faith information, allow trading scams or offer off-platform currency deals. Review current [Advertising Standards](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/13722260778260-Advertising-Standards) before any spending, including rules relevant to religious themes. Paid scaling requires measured LTV/CAC and an explicit owner budget.

## Roadmap and update cadence
Timelines below are relative to readiness gates, not promised release dates.

| Stage | Scope | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| MVP now | Three regions, charge breaks, character progression, protected saves, optional reading, build tooling | Cloud checks; engine tests still pending |
| Closed testing | 12–20 observed testers; mobile/controller and two-client sessions | No destructive save/reward bugs; understandable onboarding |
| Open beta | Authored recovery variation, final asset pass, mature content declaration, validated private services | Measured return behavior and stable operation |
| Initial release | Truthful listing, reviewed cosmetics, support/moderation process | Owner authorization and actual publishing evidence |
| First 30 days | Weekly fixes; one small encounter variation if data supports it | Review cohorts, complaints, failures and conversion |
| First 90 days | Route variants and first earned equipment interaction set | Demonstrated demand; positive marginal economics |
| Six months | Cooperative expeditions and authored settlement stories | Sustainable content/support capacity |
| Twelve months | Additional harbors and optional creator collaboration | Preserve identity and measured profitability |

Suggested first-month operations: week 1 diagnose onboarding and saves; week 2 fix the highest-friction combat step; week 3 trial a small route variation; week 4 evaluate cohort return and fair cosmetic value. Keep optional faith material stable except for reviewed clarity/corrections. Do not create a weekly content treadmill before capacity is known.

Review monthly: actual feedback, onboarding funnel, D1/D7/D30 by channel, session distributions, spending/refunds, save health and device performance. Stop paid promotion if acquired cohorts do not return. Prioritize one player problem per update and compare outcomes honestly. No synthetic feedback or fabricated engagement.
